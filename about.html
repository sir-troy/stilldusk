About Stilldusk

A calm focus timer for deep work.

What it does

Stilldusk is a quiet, dusk-coloured focus timer. It follows the familiar rhythm of focus sessions and short and long breaks, with soft soundscapes, calming atmospheres, different clock faces and a Zen mode that hides everything but the time. It's designed to help you start, stay and finish — without noise.

How to use it

  1. Pick Focus, Short break or Long break. Drag the ring (or scroll) to fine-tune the time.
  2. Type what you're focusing on, if you like.
  3. Press play — or Space on your keyboard. R resets, S skips, Z toggles Zen mode.
  4. Open Sounds, Theme, Clock and Rhythm in the dock to make it yours.

Why I made this

I don't trust most “free” tools online.

Most free websites that convert a PDF or crunch your numbers do it on their own servers. Your files and data leave your device, and you have to trust that they're deleted afterwards. Very few of them can honestly promise zero data retention.

I use these every day — so I made my own.

These are tools I actually need in my day-to-day work and life. Instead of uploading my stuff to a stranger's server every time, I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to upload, nothing to retain.

Made for me, shared with you. Why not?

I built them for personal use, but there's no reason to keep them to myself. If they're useful to me, they might be useful to you too — free, no sign-up, and your data stays with you.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see the time in another tab?

Yes. While the timer runs, the remaining time is shown in the browser tab title, so you can glance at it from anywhere.

What does Zen mode do?

It fades out everything except the timer so nothing distracts you.

Are my sessions tracked?

Only on your device. Today's session count and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage.

Your privacy, in short

Stilldusk does its work in your browser. Your settings (sounds, theme, clock face, rhythm) and today's session count are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
