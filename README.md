# Stilldusk

A calm focus timer for deep work.

**Live:** https://stilldusk.useful-indeed.com

Stilldusk is a quiet, dusk-coloured focus timer. It follows the familiar rhythm of focus sessions and short and long breaks, with soft soundscapes, calming atmospheres, different clock faces and a Zen mode that hides everything but the time. It's designed to help you start, stay and finish — without noise.

## How to use

1. Pick Focus, Short break or Long break. Drag the ring (or scroll) to fine-tune the time.
2. Type what you're focusing on, if you like.
3. Press play — or Space on your keyboard. R resets, S skips, Z toggles Zen mode.
4. Open Sounds, Theme, Clock and Rhythm in the dock to make it yours.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your settings (sounds, theme, clock face, rhythm) and today's session count are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.
