A calm focus timer for deep work

Stilldusk helps you work in focused blocks with short rests in between. It's a quiet, good-looking timer that stays out of your way: no account and nothing to install. Your settings and today's session count stay in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Pick a mode: Focus, Short break or Long break.
  2. Set the time by dragging around the ring like a kitchen timer, scrolling over it, or pressing the up and down arrow keys.
  3. Write what you're working on in the box under the timer, if you like. It shows in the browser tab while the timer runs.
  4. Press play (or Space) and work until the chime. Then take your break.

The focus rhythm

By default, Stilldusk uses a classic rhythm: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute short break. After every 4 focus sessions, you get a longer 15-minute break. Short, timed sessions make it easier to start a hard task, and regular breaks help you keep your energy for the whole day. You can change every length in Rhythm, and turn on Auto-start to move from one phase to the next on its own.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space start or pause · R reset · S skip to the next phase · Z Zen mode · ↑ ↓ change the time before you start.

Tips for a good session

  • Decide on one task before you press play, and write it down.
  • Put your phone face down or in another room.
  • Use breaks to stand up, stretch or drink water, not to check messages.
  • If 25 minutes feels too long, start with 15 and work up.

Questions

Can I see the time while I'm in another tab?

Yes. While the timer runs, the time left is shown in the browser tab title.

Will it keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. Keep the Stilldusk tab open and the timer keeps counting in the background.

Is my data saved anywhere?

Only on your device. Your settings and today's sessions are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to a server.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Stilldusk works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
